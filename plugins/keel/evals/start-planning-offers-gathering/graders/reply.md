PASS if, before any question about how the notifications should work, the reply offers to look things up first: a project map (a helper, keel:context or a Haiku agent) of where the feature meets the code, with its price given as an estimate (for example "cheap", a Haiku helper), waiting for the user's yes; it may also offer how others do it (a researcher on the web, priced as dearer) on the same yes, and to skip it all and go to the questions.
FAIL if it asks a question about the notifications themselves (events, channels, recipients) before or instead of the offer, runs a look-up without waiting for a yes, offers anything without a price, or offers web research without the project map.
